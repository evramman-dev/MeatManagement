This corpus preserves the 27 New Testament source books and the source chapter/verse labels. It is independent from the Brenton Septuagint and from the SBL Greek New Testament.
Processing: USFM structural markers and footnotes/cross-references were removed for reader display. Greek source text and source verse labels are retained. No missing verse or book is filled from another edition.
The edition represents the Byzantine / Majority Text form. It is not presented as the SBLGNT or as a Nestle-Aland edition.
