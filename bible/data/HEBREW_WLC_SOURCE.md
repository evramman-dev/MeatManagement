# Hebrew Westminster Leningrad Codex

Translation ID: HE-WLC-2017
Edition: Westminster Leningrad Codex, Biblical Hebrew (hboWLC)
Source download: https://ebible.org/Scriptures/hboWLC_usfm.zip
Source information: https://ebible.org/find/details.php?id=hboWLC
Source repository and attribution: https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb
License of the WLC biblical text: Public Domain.

The WLC text itself is public domain. The source package also describes separate lemma and morphology data under CC BY-SA 3.0. This application imports only the Hebrew biblical text and excludes lemma, morphology, and Strong's data.

The conversion removes USFM structural markers, footnotes, and cross-reference notes. It preserves Hebrew text, vowel points, cantillation, and source verse references. No wording changes are intended.

Coverage: 39 Masoretic Old Testament books. This edition does not contain the Coptic Orthodox Deuterocanonical books. Their absence is recorded as edition coverage, not as a change to the application's Coptic canon.


## Coverage and versification notes

The source archive contains 39 Masoretic books and 23,213 verse records. Its book-level audit records Esther as 10 chapters, Daniel as 12 chapters, and Psalms as 150 psalms. It does not contain the Greek additions to Esther or Daniel, Psalm 151, or the Deuterocanonical books. The app keeps these source limits visible and does not fill them from the Septuagint or another edition.
